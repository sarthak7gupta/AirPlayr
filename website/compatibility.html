Media, Codec, Platform & Receiver Compatibility

AirPlayr v1.6.0 supports macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS/iPadOS, Android, and Web—combining hardware decoders with zero-re-encode 1080p/4K fMP4 HLS passthrough and a sandboxed LGPL FFmpeg 9.0.2 remux bridge.

Wireless Receivers & Protocols

Apple AirPlay & AirPlay 2

Uses Apple’s native AVRoutePickerView for Apple TV, HomePod, and AirPlay 2 smart TVs. Automatically switches to a full-duration Zero-Re-Encode 1080p/4K VOD HLS stream when a receiver rejects the source container or codec tag.

Google Chromecast

Discovers Chromecast receivers on your LAN via Bonjour (_googlecast._tcp) and controls playback over CastV2 TLS/Protobuf on port 8009 (LAUNCH, LOAD, PLAY, PAUSE, SEEK, STOP).

Miracast / DLNA Renderers

Automatic UDP SSDP multicast discovery (239.255.255.250:1900) + UPnP AVTransport:1 XML parsing and SOAP control, manual IP/port connection, and one-click Wireless Display shortcut.

Audio, Subtitles & Multi-Platform Support

  • Standalone Audio Formats: Native playback and wireless audio routing for WAV, AAC, M4A, MP3, AIFF, CAF, and FLAC with embedded cover artwork display.
  • Multi-Language Audio Tracks: Switch between embedded audio tracks (AAC, AC3, EAC3, MP3, FLAC, Opus, Vorbis, ALAC, PCM) with persisted preferred audio language in UserDefaults.
  • Subtitles & Captions: Auto-loads matching <movie>.srt / .ass / .ssa files, extracts embedded MKV/MP4 subtitle tracks, supports live ±0.25s sync offset, and offers opt-in OpenSubtitles search.
  • Apple Ecosystem Focus: Built natively for macOS and iOS/iPadOS, plus client-side in-browser streaming via the Web Player (experimental Windows/Linux/Android builds are hosted on the alpha branch).
