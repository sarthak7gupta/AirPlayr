04 // Subtitle Engine Specifications

FORMATS // RASTERIZATION & WEBVTT
SUB.01

SubRip (.srt)

Embedded or sidecar files. Streamed natively as segmented WebVTT tracks or rendered client-side on the cinema HUD with zero latency.

SUB.02

Advanced SubStation (.ass)

Preserves author typography, positioning coordinates (\pos), dialogue colors, and karaoke tags with in-stream raster compositing.

SUB.03

Live Sync Stepper

Granular ±0.25s audio/subtitle sync offset adjustment in the macOS toolbar, persistent across playback sessions.
