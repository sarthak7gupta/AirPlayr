Zero Friction Workflow

From your device to your TV in three seconds.

01

Drop Any Movie or Audio File

Drag in an .mkv, .mp4, .m4v, .mov, or audio file (FLAC, WAV, M4A, MP3). Matching .srt subtitles in the same folder load automatically.

02

Pick Your TV

Choose your Apple TV, AirPlay smart TV, Chromecast, or Miracast/DLNA display. AirPlayr handles format compatibility automatically in the background.

03

Scrub, Switch Tracks & Relax

Seek anywhere on the full movie timeline from your device or TV remote, switch audio languages, adjust playback speed (0.5×–4×), and enjoy uninterrupted playback.
