01 // Protocol Routing Matrix

SPECIFICATION // MULTICAST & WIRE PROTOCOLS
PROTO.01

Apple AirPlay 2

Native AVRoutePickerView integration. Automatically discovers Apple TV 4K, HomePod stereo pairs, and AirPlay-enabled receivers with millisecond hardware synchronization.

PROTO.02

Chromecast (CastV2)

Direct Protobuf socket transport over TLS port 8009. Communicates natively with Chromecast with Google TV, Chromecast Ultra, and Nest Hubs without Google Play Services.

PROTO.03

Miracast & DLNA

Auto-SSDP multicast discovery on port 1900. Controls Smart TV renderers (LG WebOS, Samsung Tizen, Sony Bravia) using standards-compliant UPnP AVTransport:1 XML control actions.
